# Final scientific audit: 1991–2025 migration

Status: **scientific evidence gate passed with claim restrictions; production remains locked**.

This is the final post-migration code and evidence audit. It is not independent
expert certification and it does not replace the operator's legal, accessibility,
performance or domain approvals.

## Migration integrity

- 315 selected ERA5-Land model cells are present.
- Every cell carries 306,864 padded hourly observations for the approved request
  window.
- All 3,780 destination-months use `observation-validity-v1` and contain all 35
  calendar years from 1991 through 2025.
- Every published month has complete scoring inputs and data completeness `1`.
- The public manifest identifies the result as a project-defined 1991–2025
  historical climate average, explicitly not a WMO standard normal.
- The dataset remains `provisional`, so robots, sitemap and page metadata keep it
  out of search indexes until the remaining non-scientific release gates pass.

## Decision checks

- The corrected 1991–2025 comparison remains accepted: mean absolute score change
  `0.3572`, maximum absolute change `3.5803`, 17 explained eligibility changes,
  no hold changes and no monthly number-one ranking changes.
- The period-specific Golden Case gate passes with 34 agreements, 1 partial
  agreement, 0 disagreements, 6 held/no-answer cases and 5 accepted deviations.
- Unvalidated ERA5-Land grid wind remains excluded from scores, gates and best
  months.
- All 30 independent precipitation review destinations remain withheld from
  recommendations. Three persistent-snow destinations are also withheld.
- The season-alignment calibration retains the expert baseline because the
  training optimum did not improve the untouched validation subset.

## Hunza and independent diagnostic

Hunza's 1991–2025 September-to-October ERA5-Land cell change is `−13.6 °C`.
The independent NASA POWER diagnostic has the same direction but a smaller
`−6.2 °C` change. The ERA5-Land value remains unchanged and visibly warned; its
magnitude must not be described as station-validated or representative of the
whole Hunza Valley.

The independent NASA POWER snapshot covers 1991–2020, while the primary release
now covers 1991–2025. It therefore remains a conservative coarse-model diagnostic,
not a period-matched validation. It may justify retaining caution, but it cannot
remove an existing hold or validate route, whole-destination, safety or probability
claims.

## Formal result

The scientific evidence gate passes with zero scientific production blockers for
the narrowly stated product: historical climate at one selected ERA5-Land model
cell. The following claims remain prohibited:

- whole-destination representativeness;
- route-specific conditions;
- weather forecasts;
- trail-safety or go/no-go advice; and
- empirically calibrated probabilities.

Overall production release remains blocked by legal/operator details,
accessibility/performance approval and the custom production domain. No scientific
approval in this audit overrides those gates.

Machine-readable evidence is generated by `scripts/validate/science-audit.ts` in
`generated/reports/science-audit.json`. The signed period decision remains in
`data-config/methodology/historical-period-1991-2025-review-v1.json`.
