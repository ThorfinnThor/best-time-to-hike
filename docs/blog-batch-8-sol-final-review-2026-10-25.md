## Overall finding

Both bilingual articles pass the final scientific and editorial audit. Every stated climate value, eligible-month calendar, best-month list and representative-cell elevation reproduces the current public records. The interpretations stay within the scope of 35-year selected-cell averages and do not turn the model into a forecast, route-opening calendar or trail-safety claim.

The two articles use different ordered content structures, answer recognisable search questions, provide practical trade-offs instead of generic “best month” claims, and link to the underlying destination pages and methodology. Both lead images are licensed, attributed and at least 2,400 pixels wide.

## Canadian Rockies review

### Claims verified

- Banff is recommendation-eligible from June through September; Jasper, Yoho and Kananaskis are eligible from July through September.
- July, August and September form the exact common eligible window.
- August is the highest-scoring month in all four public records: Banff 83, Jasper 81, Yoho 85 and Kananaskis 81.
- The August temperature, wet-day probability, precipitation total and snow-day probability values reproduce the public month records.
- Banff's June score is 62. Jasper, Yoho and Kananaskis are recommendation-ineligible in June and publish the protected maximum score of 49.
- The July-to-October transition values, including October snow-day probabilities above 81 percent, reproduce the source files.

### Interpretation verified

The article correctly presents August as the strongest historical overlap rather than a guaranteed route season. It distinguishes monthly precipitation from a dry-season claim, keeps the four different model elevations visible, and explicitly excludes trail access, wildfire smoke, lightning, river conditions, avalanche terrain, high-pass exposure and current closures from the climate evidence.

## Greece review

### Claims verified

- Samaria Gorge is recommendation-eligible in all twelve months at its selected cell; Mount Olympus is eligible from March through December; Pindus is eligible from April through November.
- April through November is the exact common eligible window.
- May values for temperature, wet-day probability, monthly precipitation, snow-day probability and score reproduce all three public records.
- July values reproduce the very dry Samaria signal, the drier Pindus summer and the wetter Mount Olympus record without treating any one result as national weather.
- Pindus reaches its highest score of 92 in September; Samaria reaches 95 in May; Mount Olympus has a three-way best-month list for July, August and September.

### Interpretation verified

The article correctly rejects one national hiking calendar. It uses May as a cross-region planning compromise and July as an example of regional divergence. Route access, gorge opening, hut schedules, wildfire restrictions, thunderstorms, water availability, exposure and current mountain conditions remain outside the model claim and are handed back to current official sources.

Those destination pages remain available to users but become `noindex` and leave the sitemap. Among the previously approved destination set, they had the two weakest combined editorial presentation signals: Canyonlands had the shortest English long-form copy, while Appenzell combined near-minimum copy depth with one of the smallest 1,200-pixel image assets. Both still satisfy the minimum floor; this is a prioritisation decision, not a scientific rejection.
