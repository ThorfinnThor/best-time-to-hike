# Licensing and attribution

The current public fixture build does not distribute third-party climate or DEM values. The following notices are prepared for the real-data release but do not auto-approve its legal gate.

## ERA5-Land

- Dataset: ERA5-Land hourly time-series data from 1950 to present
- Dataset ID: `reanalysis-era5-land-timeseries`
- DOI: `10.24381/ee82e357`
- Licence shown by the Climate Data Store: CC BY 4.0
- Provider: Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF

Use remains subject to accepting the current terms on the [official dataset page](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-land-timeseries).

## Copernicus DEM GLO-30

The required notice for adapted/derived GLO-30 data is:

> produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved

The official source also requires the applicable no-liability notice to accompany public distribution. Dataset DOI: `10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65`. See the [Copernicus DEM collection and obligations](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM).

Operator/legal approval is still required before these prepared notices are treated as complete for the product, especially for the exact placement and wording of the no-liability notice, operator identity, images, and any future OSM-derived data.
