# Final scientific audit: 315 destinations, 1991–2025

Date: 2026-10-06
Reviewer/sign-off name: ThorfinnThor
Scientific evidence status: passed with explicit claim restrictions
Overall production release: not approved by this audit

## Audited publication scope

The public release contains 315 destinations and 3,780 destination-months. Every committed climate snapshot uses the project-defined 1991–2025 historical average, contains all 35 complete calendar years, records 306,864 padded hourly observations for its selected source cell, and reports complete monthly source coverage.

The evidence supports climatology for one selected ERA5-Land model cell per destination. It does not support a whole-region average, a route-specific statement, a weather forecast, trail-safety advice, or an empirically calibrated probability.

## Decisions

1. Grid wind remains visible only as context. ERA5-Land 10 m grid-cell wind is not validated for exposed paths or gusts. Algorithm 1.3.0 therefore assigns it zero weight and excludes it from recommendation gates, score labels and best-month selection.
2. Numeric public confidence is retired. No destination or month JSON publishes `confidenceScore` or `confidenceLevel`. Missing or conflicting evidence causes a recommendation hold instead of an arbitrary score cap.
3. The independent precipitation diagnostic remains conservative. Thirty destinations with large ERA5-Land versus NASA POWER precipitation disagreement publish provenance but no score, ranking or best-month recommendation.
4. Three destinations are held because all twelve monthly source values trigger the configured persistent-snow review rule: El Chaltén, Garhwal and Zermatt.
5. The approved 31-case Golden set passes the signed 1991–2025 period review. Held destinations are explicitly excluded from agreement scoring rather than treated as successful predictions.

## Hunza anomaly

The selected ERA5-Land cell cools by 13.6 °C from September to October. The independent NASA POWER/MERRA-2 diagnostic also cools across the same transition, by 6.2 °C. This corroborates the direction but not the magnitude; neither source is station truth. The source values are retained without smoothing, the anomaly remains visible as a data-quality warning, and Hunza remains on precipitation-validation hold.
